# User Persona — Working College Student

Status: Draft based on Ian’s self-reported responses.
Peer research is pending.

## Profile
- Name: Emily
- Life situation: Returning to college while working.
- Current tool: CourseDen for tracking assignments and deadlines.

## Goals
- See deadlines from all classes in one place.
- Keep up with coursework after work.
- Have enough notice to plan and complete assignments.

## Current Behavior
Checks CourseDen to find assignments and due dates.

## Challenges
- Keeping up with coursework after work.
- Remembering due dates between CourseDen logins.
- Has missed a deadline or rushed an assignment after
  realizing it was due soon.

## Needs
- A combined view of deadlines across all classes.
- Reminders one week before, one day before, and on
  the due date.

## Design Implications
- Show upcoming deadlines together on the home screen.
- Clearly display the class and due date for each item.
- Allow multiple reminders for each deadline.
